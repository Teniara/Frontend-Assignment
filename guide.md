
# Lagos: The City That Never Sleeps 🇳🇬❤️

![Third Mainland Bridge, Lagos](https://dailytrust.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Third-Mainland-Bridge.jpg)

## A Little Introduction

Lagos is a city that never sleeps. And honestly, I don't think it knows how to.

Lagos is my state of origin. I was born and raised here, so in many ways, this city is home in its truest sense. It is the place I know, the place I grew up, and a place that has shaped so much of how I see the world.

Growing up, I never really understood why everyone wanted to come to Lagos. Why leave your hometown, your family and everything familiar just to come and struggle in a city where traffic alone can test your patience?

I didn't get it. Not until I got much older.

Then I began to understand something: **Lagos is the heart of opportunities in Nigeria.**

It is a city where people arrive with dreams, ambitions, little resources and sometimes nothing but hope. Somehow, many find a way to build something for themselves. It is a city that brings people from different backgrounds, cultures and walks of life together, all searching for something.

And that, to me, is one of the most fascinating things about Lagos.

## The City Everyone Has Something to Say About

Now, let's be honest. Lagos is not without its problems.

You will hear people say:

- Lagos is smelling.
- Lagos is overrated.
- Lagos is overcrowded.
- Lagos traffic is unbearable.
- Everything is expensive.
- The city is simply too stressful.

And you know what? Some of these complaints are valid.

But sometimes, I wonder if people realise that quite a number of these complaints also apply to cities like New York.

New York has its traffic, congestion, high cost of living and urban challenges. Yet, people still travel from different parts of the world to experience it, work there, build businesses and pursue their dreams.

I am not saying Lagos and New York are the same, or that Lagos has no room for improvement. Far from it.

I simply think that we sometimes become so focused on what is wrong with Lagos that we forget to appreciate what the city has made possible for so many people.

Lagos has its shortcomings, but it also has an incredible capacity to create opportunities.

## Why I Love Lagos

There are so many things I love about this city, and I could probably write an entire book about them.

### 1. The Opportunities

Lagos is a land of possibilities. Whether you are interested in business, technology, entertainment, fashion, finance, engineering or the creative industry, there is a community, a network or an opportunity waiting to be explored.

It is a place where ideas meet people, people meet opportunities, and sometimes, the most unexpected encounters change the direction of someone's life.

### 2. The People

![The People of Lagos](https://cdn.guardian.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/000_42WY9D2-1536x1024.jpg)

Lagos is a melting pot of cultures, personalities and ambitions.

You meet people from different parts of Nigeria and beyond. Everyone has a story, everyone is trying to figure something out, and everyone seems to be going somewhere.

There is something beautiful about a city where so many different people can find a place to belong.

### 3. The Energy

![Energy of Lagos](https://cdn.guardian.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/000_42WY9CT-1536x1024.jpg)

There is an energy to Lagos that is difficult to explain.

From the early morning rush to the late-night activities, the city is constantly moving. Markets are buzzing, businesses are opening, music is playing, people are hustling, and somewhere, someone is starting something new.

Even when Lagos is exhausting, it is rarely boring!

### 4. The Culture and Lifestyle

![Food](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcT9JOincokPXxOXezNloljLR8X6V9E1Iqw9TSyBNtZv3w&s=10)

The food, music, fashion, art, entertainment and celebrations make Lagos special.

From street food to fancy restaurants, traditional celebrations to music concerts, there is always something to experience.

Lagos has a personality of its own, and I absolutely love that.

## Places That Make Lagos Special

If you ever find yourself in Lagos, here are some places worth exploring:

1. **Lekki Conservation Centre:** A beautiful nature reserve with a famous canopy walkway.
2. **Tarkwa Bay Beach:** A lovely beach accessible by boat.
3. **Nike Art Gallery:** A wonderful space celebrating Nigerian art and creativity.
4. **Freedom Park:** A historical and cultural centre on Lagos Island.
5. **National Museum Lagos:** A place to explore Nigerian history and heritage.
6. **Eko Atlantic:** A modern coastal development showcasing another side of Lagos.

## A City That Keeps Giving

I know Lagos has a lot of growing to do. There are infrastructure challenges, environmental concerns, traffic problems and many other issues that deserve attention.

But I also know that this is a city that has birthed opportunities, businesses, careers, friendships, dreams and privileges for countless people.

People come to Lagos looking for a chance, and many find one.

Some come to start businesses. Others come to school, find jobs, build careers, pursue creative interests or simply create a better life for themselves and their families.

Not everyone finds what they came looking for, of course. But the possibilities are there, and that is what makes Lagos remarkable to me.

**Lagos is a city that keeps giving.**

## My Final Thoughts ❤️

Perhaps my love for Lagos is partly because I was born and raised here. Perhaps it is because this is the city whose streets, people, culture and peculiarities have become familiar to me.

Or perhaps it is because, as I grow older, I am beginning to see beyond the traffic, the noise and the everyday chaos.

I am beginning to appreciate the opportunities, the resilience, the ambition and the stories that make this city what it is.

Lagos may not be perfect, but it is home. And I will always have a soft spot for it.

If you ask me whether I love Lagos?

**I LOVE, LOVE, LOVE, LOVE LAGOS!**

*Eko o ni baje!* 🇳🇬❤️
